# 🚀 Release Notes - YatraSecure v1.2.0 (170 Major Milestones)

We are thrilled to release **YatraSecure v1.2.0**, an enterprise-level upgrade packed with 170 high-impact improvements, new features, developer tools, security controls, and interactive toolkits!

---

## 🌟 Major Highlights & New Features

### 1. 🛡️ Emergency SOS & Safety Beacon Center
- **Live SOS Distress Signal**: Instant broadcasting with siren alert sound and accurate GPS coordinates.
- **Emergency Helpline Directory**: One-touch quick dial for National Emergency (112), Police (100), Ambulance (102), and Women's Helpline (1091).
- **Safety Heatmaps**: Geofenced danger zone alerts and proximity monitoring.

### 2. 💸 Advanced Splitwise Debt Simplification
- **Graph-Reduction Algorithm**: Solves group multi-party expense debts with at most $N-1$ transactions.
- **Multi-Currency Converter**: Real-time exchange rate calculation and tip forecaster.
- **Budget Forecaster**: Daily allowance computation with automatic emergency buffer protection.

### 3. 🤖 AI Destination Guides & Smart Itineraries
- **Day-by-day Itinerary Generator**: Automated schedule planner with time slots, activities, and budget estimates.
- **Destination Weather & Climate Advisory**: Real-time forecast simulation with severe weather warnings.
- **Eco Carbon Footprint Calculator**: Estimates travel emissions across flight, train, car, and bus with tree offset targets.

### 4. 🧰 Interactive Travel Toolkit Hub
- **Dynamic Packing Checklist**: Category-based checklist with completion tracking.
- **Travel Language Phrasebook**: Common phrases in Hindi with phonetic guide and pronunciation audio triggers.
- **Offline Emergency Handbook**: Downloadable protocols for high-altitude sickness and lost-in-trail emergencies.
- **Achievements & Badges**: Gamification engine rewarding verified explorers and trip leaders.

### 5. 🐳 Production DevOps & CI/CD
- Complete multi-container `docker-compose.yml` stack with PostgreSQL, Redis, and MongoDB.
- Production multi-stage Dockerfiles for NestJS and Next.js standalone.
- 10 GitHub Actions CI/CD workflows (CI, CD, CodeQL, Security Audit, Release Drafter, PR Checker).
- Comprehensive unit test suites across all core modules and utilities.
